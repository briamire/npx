Who We Are

Comprehensive Construction Solutions

EC&L — Everlast Construction & Logistics — operates as a full-service construction partner, specialising in three core areas for contractors, developers and homeowners across Kenya.

1. Material Procurement: Strategic partnerships with certified suppliers ensure premium-grade materials at competitive prices — cement, steel, gypsum, sand, ballast, aggregates and more.

2. Logistics & Transport: Reliable tipper trucks and specialised vehicles enable just-in-time delivery from source to your construction site.

3. Gypsum Solutions: From gypsum boards and ceiling systems to full installation — we supply and install modern gypsum finishes for residential and commercial projects.
